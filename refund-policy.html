Clear, transparent refund terms for in-app subscriptions and purchases made in WhiteBoard.

1. In-App Purchase Details

WhiteBoard provides core sketching, drawing, and teaching features completely free. Users may optionally upgrade to WhiteBoard Pro:

  • Product: WhiteBoard Pro (Annual Subscription / In-App Upgrade)
  • Price: ₹49/year (or local currency equivalent in your country)
  • What It Unlocks: Completely removes all banner and interstitial ads, unlocks unlimited version snapshots, and grants access to premium studio tools.
  • Payment Processor: Google Play Billing (all transactions are processed exclusively by Google Play). Tech2Nine does not collect or store payment details.

2. Google Play Refund Policy

Because all in-app transactions are processed directly by Google Play, refund requests are governed by the official Google Play Refund Policy:

A. Within 48 Hours of Purchase (Direct Self-Service via Google Play)

If less than 48 hours have passed since your purchase, you can request a refund directly through Google Play for the fastest resolution:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your Profile Icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Select Payments & subscriptions > Budget & order history.
  4. Locate the WhiteBoard Pro transaction.
  5. Tap Request a refund or Report a problem and follow the on-screen instructions.

Alternatively, visit Google Play Order History from any web browser.

B. After 48 Hours of Purchase (Developer Support Review)

If more than 48 hours have passed, you can email our support team directly at tech2nine.dev@gmail.com with:

  • Your Google Play Order Number (e.g., GPA.1234-5678-9012-34567, found in your Google Play purchase receipt email).
  • The date of purchase.
  • A brief description of why you are requesting a refund.

We review developer refund requests within 3 business days and process eligible refunds through the Google Play Developer Console.

3. Refund Eligibility Criteria

Refunds are generally approved in the following circumstances:

  • Accidental Purchase: The purchase was made accidentally (e.g., by a family member or child).
  • Unauthorized Transaction: The purchase was unauthorized or fraudulent.
  • Technical Malfunction: A verified technical issue in the App prevents you from accessing Pro features, and our support team is unable to resolve it.

Refunds may be declined if:

  • The refund request is submitted long after the purchase date without a valid technical reason.
  • There is clear evidence of abuse, fraud, or repeated refund requests.

4. What Happens After a Refund

  • Pro Status Revoked: Your account status will revert to the standard Free tier.
  • Your Drawings Are 100% Safe: Refunding Pro will NEVER delete, modify, or affect any of your locally saved whiteboards, drawings, PDF exports, or notes. Your content remains safely on your device.
  • Processing Timeline: The refund amount is credited back to your original payment method in accordance with Google Play and your bank's processing timeline (typically 3–5 business days).

5. Core Free Features Notice

The core whiteboard drawing tools — including the multi-brush pen, eraser, geometric shapes, text tool, ruler, math formulas, layers, image import, PDF export, and local lesson recorder — are 100% free and do not require any purchase. The Pro upgrade is strictly an optional enhancement.
