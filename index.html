1. Core Privacy Principles (Local-First Design)

Tech2Nine ("we", "our", or "us") built the WhiteBoard application (package name: com.tech2nine.whiteboard) as a modern digital whiteboard for drawing, teaching, note-taking, and presentation. By installing or using WhiteBoard, you agree to the privacy practices outlined in this policy.

Zero Personal Data Harvesting: WhiteBoard does NOT require user account creation, registration, or login. All drawings, notes, equations, and lesson recordings stay 100% on your local device. We never sell, rent, or monetize your creative or educational work.

The core drawing, canvas tools, shapes, ruler, math formulas, science stickers, and PDF export features work completely offline without an internet connection. An internet connection is used only for loading advertisements (on the free tier), remote feature configuration flags, Google Cast streaming, and the optional Web Image Search feature.

2. No Personal Information Collected

WhiteBoard, by itself, does NOT collect, ask for, or store any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to sign up, sign in, or provide an email address, password, or username to use WhiteBoard.
  • No Personal Contact Data: We do not access or collect your real name, phone number, address, contacts, or phonebook.
  • No Biometric Extraction: While the App supports an optional Biometric Lock to secure private boards, biometric authentication is handled solely by Android's hardware security module (BiometricPrompt). The App never sees, accesses, or stores biometric data.
  • No Location Tracking: The App does not request or track precise GPS location.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All content you create inside WhiteBoard is stored exclusively on your device using Android's private app sandbox, Room database, and Jetpack DataStore:

  • Whiteboard pages, drawings, vector stroke coordinates, colors, and layers.
  • Inserted geometric shapes, LaTeX math formulas, tables, and STEM science stickers.
  • Imported images and background PDF documents.
  • App preferences (dark/light theme, toolbar layout, selected brush sizes, haptic feedback toggle).
  • Recorded lesson videos (stored in your device's local Movies/WhiteBoard/ folder).

None of this content is uploaded or transmitted to Tech2Nine servers.

5. Screen Recording & Foreground Service Disclosure

WhiteBoard includes an integrated Lesson Recorder Studio:

  • Explicit User Activation: Screen recording and presentation casting NEVER activate silently. They start only when you explicitly tap the Record or Cast button.
  • Android System Consent: Before recording begins, Android displays an official system-level dialog stating that WhiteBoard will capture your screen.
  • Persistent Notification: During recording, a persistent Android notification is always visible in your notification shade, allowing you to pause or stop recording at any moment.
  • Local Storage: Recorded MP4 lesson videos are saved directly to your device's Movies/WhiteBoard/ directory. They are never uploaded to any remote server.

6. On-Device AI & Machine Learning (ML Kit)

WhiteBoard utilizes Google Play Services ML Kit for on-device intelligent features:

  1. Smart Draw & Digital Ink Recognition: Automatically cleans rough hand-drawn circles, ellipses, triangles, and arrows into crisp geometric vectors.
  2. OCR Text Recognition: Extracts editable text from camera snapshots or image files of notes.
100% On-Device Processing: All ML Kit models execute entirely locally on your Android device hardware. No photos, camera frames, handwriting strokes, or converted text are sent to external servers or used for machine learning training.

7. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The App integrates the following Google and infrastructure services. Each is governed by its respective privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Used to serve banner and interstitial advertisements for users on the free tier. AdMob may collect device model, OS version, approximate location (IP-based), and advertising identifiers for ad measurement and fraud prevention. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Processes optional WhiteBoard Pro upgrades securely. Tech2Nine never receives, processes, or stores your payment card details, UPI IDs, or banking credentials. Google Play Terms.
  • Google Play Services & Cast SDK: Powers local wireless screen casting to smart TVs and displays.
  • Supabase: Used solely for remote feature flag synchronization (e.g., enabling/disabling app tools globally) and verifying anonymous purchase entitlements. No personal user data is stored. Supabase Privacy Policy.
No Invasive Analytics: WhiteBoard does NOT integrate Facebook SDK, invasive user tracking brokers, or behavioral spyware.

8. Optional Web Image Search (Wikimedia Commons)

WhiteBoard includes an optional Web Image Search feature that enables you to search for freely-licensed educational images and insert them into your board:

  • Data Transmitted: Only the search query text you type is sent over HTTPS to the public Wikimedia Commons API. No personal identity, user accounts, device identifiers, or location data are sent with the request.
  • Attribution & Storage: Downloaded images and thumbnail previews are saved locally onto your whiteboard canvas. They are not uploaded to our servers.
  • This feature is completely optional and activates only when you explicitly open "Web Image" and perform a search. Wikimedia Privacy Policy.

13. Data Retention & User Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's mandatory User Data Deletion policies:

How to Delete Your Data Instantly:
  1. Delete Individual Boards: In WhiteBoard, open the History or Boards tab, tap on any whiteboard, and tap Delete. This immediately deletes the drawing and undo history from local storage.
  2. Complete Data Reset: Go to your Android device Settings > Apps > WhiteBoard > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data. This permanently wipes all boards, local settings, templates, and cached files from your device.
  3. Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling WhiteBoard removes all local sandbox databases and drawing files.
  4. Server Verification Data Deletion: If you wish to request deletion of any cached diagnostic or purchase verification records linked to your device identifier, email us at tech2nine.dev@gmail.com with your device details. We will process and confirm deletion within 30 days.

14. Data Security & Offline Operation

WhiteBoard protects your data using industry-standard measures:

  • App Sandbox Protection: Your drawings are stored in Android's protected private storage area, inaccessible to other apps without your explicit export.
  • Encrypted Transport: All network traffic (such as remote configuration checks) is strictly encrypted via HTTPS/TLS 1.3.
  • Android Share Sheet: Sharing drawings to external apps (e.g., WhatsApp, Drive, Gmail) uses Android's secure FileProvider with temporary, revocable URI permissions. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external third-party apps once shared.
