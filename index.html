1. Core Privacy Principles (Local-First Design)

Tech2Nine ("we", "our", or "us") respects and values your privacy. We designed WhiteBoard (package name: com.tech2nine.whiteboard) as a modern, high-performance digital teaching and sketching board with on-device computing at its core.

Key Commitment: We do NOT harvest, sell, rent, or monetize your creative drawings, handwritten lecture notes, mathematical formulas, or recorded MP4 lessons. All drawings and notes remain strictly stored on your physical device.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed, how device hardware permissions are used, and your rights under global privacy laws including GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play Developer Policies.

2. Information Collection and Processing

A. What We DO NOT Collect:

  • No Personal Identity: We do not ask for or collect your name, home address, phone number, or contacts.
  • No Sketches or Board Content: Your whiteboards, canvases, sticky notes, formulas, and stickers are saved only in your local Room Database and internal app storage. They are never transmitted to our servers.
  • No Biometric Data: If you use biometric lock (fingerprint/face unlock) for private boards, the authentication is processed entirely within Android's hardware security module (BiometricPrompt). The app never reads or stores raw biometric templates.

B. What May Be Processed:

  • Hashed Device Identifier: An anonymized string derived from Android ID is used solely to verify active in-app Pro licenses and check remote tool configuration via Supabase.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Device model, screen orientation, and Android OS version to provide correct scaling, aspect ratio, and crash stability.
  • Advertising Identifiers (Free Users): For free users, Google AdMob may process the Google Advertising ID (GAID) to display ads in compliance with Google's Advertising Policies. Pro users have all ads completely disabled.

4. Screen Recording & Foreground Service Disclosure

WhiteBoard includes a built-in Lesson Recorder Studio and Live Screen Share system:

  • Explicit User Initiation: Screen recording and presentation projection NEVER start automatically. They start only when you explicitly tap the record or cast button.
  • System-Level Confirmation: Android displays an official system prompt informing you that WhiteBoard will capture your screen before recording begins.
  • Persistent Notification: While recording, Android displays a mandatory persistent foreground notification in your status bar indicating active capture.
  • Storage Location: Recorded MP4 lesson videos are saved directly to your local device directory (Movies/WhiteBoard/). They are NOT uploaded to any cloud server.

5. On-Device AI & Machine Learning (ML Kit)

WhiteBoard utilizes Google Play Services ML Kit for two intelligent features:

  1. OCR (Optical Character Recognition): Extracts editable text from images of printed or handwritten notes.
  2. Digital Ink Recognition & Smart Draw: Cleans rough hand-drawn strokes into perfect geometric shapes (rectangles, ellipses, arrows, stars).
100% On-Device Processing: ML Kit models run entirely locally on your Android device hardware. No photos, camera frames, handwriting strokes, or converted texts are sent to external servers or used for machine learning training.

6. Third-Party Services and SDKs

To deliver specific functionalities, WhiteBoard integrates the following reputable third-party SDKs, which may collect information governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Core application runtime, ML Kit, and Cast Framework. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: Provides banner and interstitial advertisements for users on the free tier. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers and anonymous usage data. AdMob Privacy Guide.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Processes Pro plan upgrades and subscriptions securely. Tech2Nine never accesses or stores your banking or credit card details. Google Play Terms.
  • Supabase: Used exclusively for remote feature flag synchronization (e.g., enabling/disabling app tools globally) and validating authorized purchases. Supabase Privacy Policy.

WhiteBoard is designed as a creative and educational tool suitable for students, educators, and children of all ages:

  • No social networking, public commenting, or chat rooms exist in WhiteBoard.
  • If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at support@tech2nine.com and we will promptly take steps to delete any such data.

9. Data Retention & User Data Deletion Policy

In compliance with Google Play's mandatory User Data Deletion requirements:

How to Delete Your Data Instantly:
  1. Delete Individual Boards: In WhiteBoard, open the History or Boards tab, tap on any whiteboard, and select Delete. This immediately deletes the drawing and undo history from local storage.
  2. Complete Data Reset: Go to your Android device Settings > Apps > WhiteBoard > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data. This permanently wipes all boards, local settings, templates, and cached files from your device.
  3. Server Verification Data Deletion: If you wish to request deletion of any cached diagnostic or purchase verification records linked to your device identifier, email us at support@tech2nine.com with your device details. We will process and confirm deletion within 30 days.

10. Data Security & Storage

We implement modern security practices to protect your data:

  • All network communications (such as remote feature flag queries) are transmitted using secure HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Board exports shared with other apps utilize Android's secure FileProvider with temporary, revocable URI permissions rather than direct file system paths.
  • Private locked boards leverage the Android Keystore system.
